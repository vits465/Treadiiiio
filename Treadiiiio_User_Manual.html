Treadiiiio Forex Bot
Comprehensive User Manual & Feature Guide

Version 2.0 (Realistic Edge Upgrade) | Date: July 2026

Overview: Treadiiiio is a highly advanced, risk-first Forex trading bot. It combines traditional technical indicators (SMA, RSI, Bollinger Bands) with Machine Learning (XGBoost) to generate signals. The system is protected by a multi-layered, institutional-grade risk management framework to preserve capital in all market conditions.

1. How to Start the Bot (The Monday Routine)

Forex markets operate Monday through Friday. Since the bot has strict time filters, it will automatically pause trading on weekends. Here is how you start your system for the trading week:

  1. Open your terminal and navigate to the project folder (D:\Frerlancing\Treding Bot).
  2. Start the Core Trading Engine: Type npm run dev. You will see logs indicating the database is loaded and the engine is polling prices.
  3. Start the Dashboard: Open a second terminal, go to D:\Frerlancing\Treding Bot\dashboard, and type npm run dev -- --port 3000.
  4. Start the ML Service (Optional but Recommended): If you want the Machine Learning strategy to run, open a third terminal, navigate to your Python ML folder, and start the FastAPI server (e.g., uvicorn main:app --reload).

3. The "Realistic Edge" Features (How it protects you)

This bot was recently upgraded with institutional-grade risk controls. Here is how each feature works and why it matters:

Feature 1: Dynamic Position Sizing

How it works: Instead of betting a flat 1% on every trade, the bot scales your size up or down based on two things: Market Volatility (ATR) and ML Confidence.

Why you need it: In chaotic, highly volatile markets, stop-losses are easily hit due to wide price swings. The bot automatically detects this and shrinks your lot size to protect capital. Conversely, if the ML model is very confident and the market is calm, it allocates the full normal risk.

Feature 2: The ML "Gate" (Rule Confirmation)

How it works: Even if the AI predicts a "BUY", the bot won't execute it blindly. It asks three traditional indicators (SMA Trend, RSI, Bollinger Bands) for their opinion. If none of them agree with the AI, the trade is rejected.

Why you need it: AI models can sometimes hallucinate or overfit. Forcing the AI to get "approval" from basic math guarantees you never take a trade that contradicts fundamental market momentum.

Feature 3: Bounded Loss Recovery

How it works: If you lose a trade, the bot enters "Recovery Mode" to win it back, but it is strictly bounded. It will never use Martingale (doubling down). It attempts to recover a maximum of 2 times. If it loses 3 recovery trades in a row globally, it bans itself for 24 hours.

Why you need it: Revenge trading destroys accounts. This feature gives you a mathematical chance to bounce back without risking a catastrophic death spiral.

Feature 4: Dynamic Circuit Breakers & Cooldowns

How it works:
1. Peak-Equity Breaker: If your account grows from $1,000 to $2,000, the 30% max drawdown is calculated from $2,000, not $1,000. It locks in your growth.
2. Consecutive Loss Cooldown: If you lose 5 trades in a row across any strategies, the bot puts itself in "time-out" for 4 hours.

Why you need it: Markets change regimes (e.g., from trending to choppy). If you lose 5 times in a row, your strategy is out of sync with the current market phase. The 4-hour timeout waits for the market to change behavior before risking more money.

Feature 5: Small Account Protections

How it works: If your balance is under $200, the bot forcefully downgrades your risk to "Conservative" (0.5% per trade). It also strictly checks the "Minimum Lot Cost" (0.01 lots). If a trade's stop-loss requires more money than your 0.5% budget allows, it cancels the trade instead of rounding up.

Why you need it: Small accounts are fragile. Brokers require a minimum of 0.01 lots. If your account is tiny, 0.01 lots might equal 5% risk on a wide stop-loss. The bot prevents this hidden over-leveraging.
